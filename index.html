Is it fresh?

From this bakery, not a factory.

  • Every jar is sealed and chilled, with a baked-on date on the lid.
  • A "stocked fresh today" card on the machine after every restock.
  • Sealed jars keep 30 days in the fridge, so nothing sits past its best.
  • Two layers of cake, two of icing, ready to eat. Not a mix.
How it works

Four steps, about three weeks.

  1. Tasting box

    Six jars for your team, on us. Taste it before you decide anything.

  2. Site check

    A 20 minute walk-through: power, internet and the best spot.

  3. Launch day

    We install the machine and bring the pink truck out front.

  4. Monthly report

    Jars sold, best hours, top flavor and your share, every month.
